Jiewen Lai received his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from The Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 2022. He completed his postdoctoral training at CUHK in 2023, after which he joined the Department of Electronic Engineering at CUHK as a Research Assistant Professor. In 2026, he also served as a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Stanford University.

Dr. Lai works on soft and bioinspired robotic systems, focusing on compliant mechanisms, modeling, mechatronics, sensorimotor control, and robophysical intelligence for biomedical applications. He has published, as first or corresponding author, in a range of leading venues, including Nat. Rev. Electr. Eng., Nat. Commun., IJRR, T-MECH, RA-L, and ICRA, among others.

Dr. Lai was a recipient of the Silver Medal of the 51st International Exhibition of Inventions Geneva (2026), the SYP Award of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society (2025), the T.J. Tarn Best Paper Award at IEEE ROBIO (2025), the Dr. Barbara Kwok Young Postdoctoral Researcher Award (2023), the IdeaBooster Fund Award (2022), and the Best Paper Finalist Award at IEEE ROBIO (2019). He also serves as an Associate Editor for ICRA (2024, 2025, 2026) and as an Early Career Editorial Board member for Biomimetic Intelligence and Robotics (Q1, IF: 5.4). He is a Member of the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (RAS) and a Senior Member of the Chinese Mechanical Engineering Society (CMES).
